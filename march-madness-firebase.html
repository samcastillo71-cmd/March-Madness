HOW TO FILL OUT YOUR BRACKET

1
Sign in — Use your school Google account. Your bracket is saved automatically.
2
Start with Round 1 — You'll see 64 teams. Click the team you think will win each game. Work from the outside in.
3
Keep going round by round — The winner of each game advances to the next round. Pick all the way to the Championship!
4
Save before the deadline — Your picks lock automatically at the deadline shown at the top of the page. You can change picks any time before then.
5
Watch your score update — As games are played, correct picks turn green and wrong ones turn red in real time. Check the Standings to see where you rank!

BRACKET COLORS EXPLAINED

🟠 Orange — your current pick. The tournament hasn't determined a winner here yet.

🟢 Green ✅ — your pick won! Points added to your score.
Wrong
🔴 Strikethrough ❌ — your pick lost. No points for this one.

⚫ Faded — team was eliminated in a different matchup. You can't pick them anymore.
Winner
🏆 Gold — the official tournament winner for this game.

TIPS & STRATEGY

💡
Check the Team Info tab — it has stats, players to watch, and injury news for every team to help you decide.
💡
1-seeds win a lot but upsets happen — In the Round of 64, a #1 seed has never lost to a #16 seed... until recently. #12 seeds beat #5 seeds about 35% of the time.
💡
Championship pick is worth 32 points — that's as many as all 32 Round of 64 games combined. Choose your champion wisely!
💡
Change picks before the deadline — not happy with your choices? You can come back and update right up until the deadline.
